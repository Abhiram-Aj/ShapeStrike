Line up three matching shapes to score. Outsmart your rivals on a massive grid. Last player standing with the most lines wins.

How To Play

Objective

Place shapes on the grid. Form 3 of the same shape in a row — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal — to score a point.

Turns

Players take turns one at a time. Click any empty cell to place your shape. The board fills up until someone wins.

Fixed Mode

Each player picks one shape before the game. That shape is yours for the entire match.

Free Mode

No assigned shapes. Each turn, choose which shape to place from all available shapes — adds a tactical layer.

Scoring

Completing a line of 3 matching shapes scores 1 point — even if the same cell is part of multiple lines.

Winner

When the board is full, the player with the most points wins. Ties are possible — fight for every line!
